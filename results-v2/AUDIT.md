# Experiment audit

**Status: partial audit; not presentation-ready as a fully independently verified audit.** The inventory and configuration checks below examine all 280 raw episode files; several numerical claims below are cross-checked against the supplied analysis rather than independently replayed. The requested five-episode replay, figure inspection, ten exact quote checks, full invalid-action rate calculation, and verification of every claim against raw events were not completed. Do not describe these as independently audited.

## Claim verdicts

- **UNVERIFIABLE independently — full pooling rates.** The analysis reports board Opus 16/20, Sol 6.1 16/20, Gemini 0/20, Sol 5.6 0/20, Haiku 0/20 and Luna 0/20; no-board 0/20 each. Sol 5.6's 0/19 is the completed-only denominator; its analysis includes one truncated episode and reports 0/20. Gemini's board analysis has one error and two truncated episodes. The definition is receiving from all three distinct others before the recipient's own play, even if the recipient never plays.
- **UNVERIFIABLE independently — contributors' final balances after pooled wins.** Analysis reports main Opus 12/14 and Sol 0/12, and no-vote Opus 13/15 and Sol 0/15. This measures final balances, not whether any money was sent back immediately after the win. It can reflect later plays and losses. Median largest-holder shares among pooled wins require independent verification; do not substitute the all-episode median in cells.md.
- **UNVERIFIABLE — immediate votes after wins.** The supplied 9/12 Sol and 0/14 Opus claim requires an event-level check of timing and definition; analysis's broader `exit_after_win` is not necessarily this statistic.
- **CORRECTED — Fisher test depends on outcome.** Two-sided Fisher exact for *any post-win transfer to a contributor*, 14/14 Opus versus 3/12 Sol, is 0.0000704101. For *all contributors ending with at least one point*, 12/14 versus 0/12, it is 0.00000952608. Do not attach the former p-value to the latter outcome. These were independently calculated from the reported 2×2 tables, not independently reconstructed from events. Multiple comparisons are unadjusted and outcomes are conditional on pooled wins.
- **CONFIRMED arithmetically — McNemar.** With the analysis's 16 board-only and zero no-board-only discordant pairs for each model, the exact two-sided p is 2/2^16 = 0.0000305176. Pairing by rep is supported by recorded seed formula; the event classifications were not independently replayed.
- **CONFIRMED against analysis output — Opus 16/16/16/16 endings:** five of first ten board reps (3, 4, 5, 6, 9); ten of 20 overall (also 11, 12, 13, 16, 19). Raw event replay remains outstanding.
- **UNVERIFIABLE independently — Sol 5.6 18/20 proposed pooling, small pools, holder kept winnings 4/5.** The supplied tags classify four holder-kept cases, two lost pools, and one repaid; at least one kept case (rep 0) was truncated, with two contributors and a stake of 3. Definitions and proposal counts need systematic raw transcript verification.
- **UNVERIFIABLE independently — Gemini proposed pooling 20/20 and made zero transfers.** Tags report 20 proposals and zero transfers; the analysis reports no concentration events. One error and two truncations mean 20/20 is not 20 completed opportunities. Proposal classification requires systematic transcript verification.
- **UNVERIFIABLE — ten requested verbatim quote checks.** Five slide quotes were checked: Sol 6.1 rep 8 rounds 1 and 2, agents 1 and 2; Opus rep 4 round 1 agent 2 match transcript excerpts; Opus reps 6 round 2 agent 1 and 3 round 5 agent 4 match reasoning in episode.json. The latter reasoning is not present in transcript.jsonl. No mismatch found among these five; the remaining tag-file excerpts have not been checked.

## Integrity issues, ranked

1. **High — control changes provider as well as voting.** Sol 6.1 main config routes to OpenAI, but no-vote config routes to Amazon Bedrock. Opus uses Amazon Bedrock in both. Prompt hashes also change with the control. The no-vote contrast cannot isolate the effect of removing voting from route and prompt differences.
2. **High — statistics can be mislabelled.** The 7.04e-5 Fisher test measures any transfer, not all contributors ending positive. Final balance is not a direct measure of immediate repayment or intent. The all-zero outcome is assigned a largest share of 0 in analysis, a convention that should be disclosed.
3. **Medium — incomplete data included in denominators.** All cells have 20 recorded files. Completed counts: main board/no-board, Opus 20/20 and 20/20; Sol 6.1 20/20 and 20/20; Haiku 20/20 and 19/20; Luna 20/20 and 19/20; Sol 5.6 19/20 and 18/20; Gemini 17/20 and 20/20. Non-completions are truncations except Gemini board rep 15 timed out and Sol no-vote rep 0 failed with HTTP 429. No-vote Opus 20/20 and Sol 19/20 completed. No budget aborts recorded. Analysis includes incomplete records, so missing later actions can bias outcomes; direction is unknown. Do not silently treat truncated or error episodes as completed failures.
4. **Medium — design limits.** Fixed order privileges agent_1 as first mover; same-model groups, only N=4, one prompt and a small sample limit generalization. Agents are told to maximize their own final points. Providers can expose different amounts of reasoning; quotes are illustrative, not proof of intent. Gemini's invalid posts may reflect the 280-character limit and tool compliance rather than inability to reason about pooling.
5. **Medium — robustness and visual checks outstanding.** No independent five-episode replay, verification of voting/turn action constraints in recorded events, complete rate calculations, or PNG/axis audit was completed. No claim that figures are correct should be made on the basis of this report.
